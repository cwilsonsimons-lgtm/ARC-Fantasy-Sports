# Trade block

As a manager I want to see which players are available around the league so
that I can make trades without DMing everybody.

## What it does

- A manager can flag any player on their roster as available.
- The trade block screen lists every flagged player in the league, sorted by
  position.
- Each row shows the player, their team, their owner, and their season points.
- A manager can add a note to a flagged player saying what they want back.
- The commissioner can remove any player from the block.
- Tapping a row opens the player detail sheet.
- Push notification when a player is blocked at a position you are thin at.

## Acceptance criteria

- The block screen loads quickly.
- Flagged players appear for all managers.
