Royal Rumble Lottery

Every ball in the machine is a team. Start at entry #30 with every team holding all their balls, draw one, and that team gets #30 and loses a ball. Then draw for #29, #28, and so on down to #1. Then the Rumble decides the order: the last team with an entrant still in gets the 1st pick.
